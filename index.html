🎧 Live Audio Visualizer

Visualize whatever audio is playing on your computer — YouTube, Spotify, anything. No uploads, nothing to embed. Just share the audio source and watch it move.

Click Start, then in the picker choose the browser tab / window / screen that's playing audio, and make sure "Share audio" (or "Share tab audio") is checked.

Idle — no audio source connected.

Runs entirely in your browser. Audio never leaves your device.
